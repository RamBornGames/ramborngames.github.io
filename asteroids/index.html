CASTING INTO THE STARFIELD

Add Asteroids to your Home Screen

Launch straight into the arcade from the Asteroids icon.

  1. Open Share in your browser toolbar. It may be under More.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.

Can’t find Add to Home Screen? Open the link in Safari and check Share → Edit Actions.

  1. Open the browser menu ⋮.
  2. Look for Install or Add to Home screen, then follow the prompts.

If your browser has no install option, open the link in Chrome.

Open Asteroids from your Home Screen once the icon appears.
